About
"I'm someone with a question I can't put down."

Hi, I'm Ritul. I'm a master's student in Delhi, and I have questions about almost everything. But one question has followed me since I was a child: God.

I've been a Shiv devotee for as long as I can remember. And for just as long, I've wondered if he is really there. So instead of pushing the question away, I decided to go looking, through psychology, philosophy, history, anthropology, and the spiritual side too.

I'm not a priest, and I'm not here to take anyone's faith away. If you've ever stood in a temple, a church or a mosque and wondered what is really going on, join me. Let's work it out together.
